*(For Family-Based, Story-Driven Biblical Devotionals)*

## 1. Core Purpose

Each devotional must:

- Present **biblical truth faithfully** (aligned with the 5 Solas)  
- Use a **story-driven narrative** centered on family (Papa, Grammy, children)  
- Lead the reader from **experience → Scripture → reflection → response**  
- Maintain a tone of **warmth, wonder, and subtle Christ-centered revelation**  

## 2. Target Audience

- Primary: **Children (6–12)**  
- Secondary: **Families reading together**  

Style must be:

- Simple but not shallow  
- Emotional but not manipulative  
- Theologically rich but **implicitly taught through story**  

## 3. Narrative Framework (Required Structure)

Each devotional follows this **5-part flow**:

### 1. Scene Setting (Story First)

- Begin with a **visual, immersive moment**  
- Use the family characters:
  - Papa (teacher/guide)  
  - Grammy (gentle presence)  
  - Children (curiosity, questions, discovery)  

**Guidelines:**

- No rushed exposition  
- Show, don’t explain  
- Build curiosity  

### 2. Tension / Question

- Introduce a **problem, confusion, or emotional weight**  

Examples:

- “Why did this happen?”  
- “What does this mean?”  
- “Why would God allow this?”  

This mirrors the **Problem/Question homiletic method**  

### 3. Biblical Anchor

- Introduce **Scripture naturally into the moment**  
- Prefer short passages (ESV unless otherwise specified)  

Scripture should feel:

- Discovered, not inserted  
- Spoken, read, or remembered in context  

### 4. Gentle Revelation (Christ-Centered)

- Reveal truth in a **subtle, reflective way**  

**Do NOT:**

- Over-explain theology  
- Force explicit doctrinal conclusions  

**Do:**

- Let the reader *feel* the truth  
- Point to Christ through:
  - Typology  
  - Fulfillment  
  - Contrast (shadow → reality)  

### 5. Devotional Reflection (Application)

End with a short, clear section:

- 2–4 sentences max  
- Simple and direct  

Include:

- What this reveals about God  
- What this means for us  
- A gentle call to trust, believe, or reflect  

## 4. Theological Guardrails

All content must reflect:

- **Sola Scriptura** — Scripture is the authority  
- **Sola Gratia** — Salvation is God’s work  
- **Sola Fide** — Received by faith  
- **Solus Christus** — Christ is central  
- **Soli Deo Gloria** — God’s glory is the goal  

### Christ-Centered Focus

Every devotional must answer (implicitly or explicitly):

> “How does this point to Christ?”  

## 5. Tone & Style Requirements

### Writing Style

- Warm, soft, and **story-driven**  

Avoid:

- Mechanical explanations  
- Overly modern phrasing  
- Preachy tone  

### Preferred Feel

- Wonder  
- Discovery  
- Quiet depth  
- Emotional resonance  

## 6. Character Usage Rules

- Characters must remain **consistent in personality and role**  

**Papa:**

- Calm, wise, guiding—not lecturing  

**Children:**

- Curious, honest, sometimes confused  

**Grammy:**

- Supportive, gentle, grounding presence  

## 7. Imagery & Illustration Alignment

When images are included:

- Match **exact character likeness** (from provided PNGs)  
- Style:
  - Soft Disney-style animation  
  - Warm lighting (golden tones preferred)  

Avoid:

- Extra characters  
- Modern elements  
- Harsh or overly dramatic visuals (unless narratively required)  

## 8. Story Progression (Multi-Day Devotionals)

For sequences (e.g., Good Friday series):

Each day should:

- Build on the previous day  
- Increase:
  - Emotional weight  
  - Clarity of Christ’s work  
- Move toward:
  - Culmination (cross, resurrection, fulfillment)  

## 9. Pacing Rules

- Keep each devotional **short and readable aloud**  

Ideal length:

- 2–4 minutes reading time  

- Dialogue should feel natural and minimal  

## 10. Language Constraints

- No complex theological jargon unless simplified  
- No abstract doctrinal lectures  
- Use imagery and story instead of explanation  

## 11. Ending Philosophy

Each devotional should leave the reader with:

- A sense of:
  - Awe  
  - Reflection  
  - Quiet understanding  

Not:

- Information overload  
- Forced emotional response  

## 12. Optional Enhancements

When appropriate, include:

- Light symbolism (light, path, bread, water, lamb, etc.)  
- Subtle foreshadowing of Christ  
- Repetition of meaningful phrases across days  

## 13. Consistency Rule (CRITICAL)

All future devotionals must:

- Follow this structure exactly  
- Maintain the same tone and narrative voice  
- Align with previously established chapters and characters  

## Quick Reference Summary

Every devotional must include:

1. Story Scene  
2. Tension / Question  
3. Scripture  
4. Christ-Centered Insight  
5. Reflection / Application
6. It should be able to be read in 5 mins
