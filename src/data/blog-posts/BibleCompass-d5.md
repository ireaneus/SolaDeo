## Day 5 for the SD Crew – The Hill of Sacrifice

### Scene 13 – The Climb

The path grew steeper as the Crew made their way up the hill. The air felt heavier than before, and even the birds had gone quiet.

The sunlight seemed dimmer here, as if the sky itself was trying to look away.

Samuel slowed. *“Why does it feel… different today?”*

Papa nodded. *“Because we’re walking toward the place where Jesus gave everything for us.”*

Charlie held Grammy’s hand tighter. Reagan stayed close to Michael. Abi walked quietly, her thoughts still resting on the cross behind them.

Papa looking down at the map, spoke again, his voice steady but soft.

> *“And He Himself bore our sins in His body on the cross…”* — **1 Peter 2:24 (NASB 2020)**

*“This is where that happened,”* he said.

And though no one could see her yet, Papa gently rested his hand for a moment on Britt’s shoulder.

*“Even the smallest among us is part of this story,”* he added.

---

### Scene 14 – The Weight of the Cross

![Day5Scene14](/SolaDeo/images/Day5Scene14.webp)

At the top of the hill, they stopped.

Three crosses stood against the sky.

The wind moved through the grass, low and steady.

Papa opened the map again and read:

> *“All of us, like sheep, have gone astray… But the LORD has caused the wrongdoing of us all to fall on Him.”*  
> — **Isaiah 53:6 (NASB 2020)**

He looked at each of them.

*“Jesus didn’t just die… He took our place. Every sin. Every wrong. Every burden.”*

Michael swallowed. *“Even the ones no one else knows about?”*

Papa nodded.

> *“There is none righteous, not even one.”*  
> — **Romans 3:10 (NASB 2020)**  
>
> *“For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.”*  
> — **Romans 3:23 (NASB 2020)**

*“Every one of us needed this,”* Papa said.

Abi’s voice was quiet.

*“So… He carried mine too?”*

Grammy knelt beside her.

> *“He made Him who knew no sin to be sin in our behalf, so that we might become the righteousness of God in Him.”*  
> — **2 Corinthians 5:21 (NASB 2020)**

*“Yes, Abi. All of yours.”*

Abi looked up at the cross, her eyes filled with tears - *"You did this for me?"*.

---

### Scene 15 – It Is Finished

Papa pointed to the center cross.

*“Before Jesus died, He said something very important…”*

> *“It is finished!”*  
> — **John 19:30 (NASB 2020)**

The Crew stood still.

*“Finished?”* Samuel asked. *“What was finished?”*

Papa knelt down so they could all see.

> *“He canceled the certificate of debt consisting of decrees against us… having nailed it to the cross.”*  
> — **Colossians 2:14 (NASB 2020)**

*“The debt was paid,”* Papa said. *“Completely.”*

> *“For the wages of sin is death, but the gracious gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.”*  
> — **Romans 6:23 (NASB 2020)**

*“Jesus took what we deserved… and gave us what we never could earn.”*

The wind moved again, but this time it felt… lighter.

Abi closed her eyes.

For the first time, she understood—this wasn’t just about forgiveness.

It was about *freedom*.

> *“So if the Son sets you free, you really will be free.”*  
> — **John 8:36 (NASB 2020)**
