## Day 6 for the SD Crew – The Empty Tomb

### Scene 16 – The Morning Light

![Day6Scene16](/SolaDeo/images/Day6Scene16.webp)

The next morning, everything felt different.

Golden light broke over the hills as the Crew made their way forward.

The darkness was gone.

The air felt fresh… alive.

As Charlie danced in the sun beams, Samuel ran ahead. *“It doesn’t feel heavy anymore!”*

Papa smiled. *“That’s because the story didn’t end at the cross.”*

> *“Weeping may last for the night, but a shout of joy comes in the morning.”*  
> — **Psalm 30:5 (NASB 2020)**

Michael looked up at the rising sun.

*“It feels like… something new.”*

---

### Scene 17 – The Stone Rolled Away

![Day6Scene17](/SolaDeo/images/Day6Scene17.webp)

They came to a small garden. At its center was a tomb carved into the rock.

But the stone…

…was rolled away.

Samuel stepped forward slowly. *“Papa… where is He?”*

Papa opened the map once more and read:

> *“Why are you seeking the living One among the dead? He is not here, but He has risen.”*  
> — **Luke 24:5–6 (NASB 2020)**

The words seemed to echo in the morning air.

Michael’s face lit up. *“That means… He won!”*

Charlie looked up and pointed to the clouds and said *"There He is, up there"*

Papa nodded.

> *“Death has been swallowed up in victory.”*  
> — **1 Corinthians 15:54 (NASB 2020)**
> *“O death, where is your victory? O death, where is your sting?”*  
> — **1 Corinthians 15:55 (NASB 2020)**

*“Sin didn’t win. Death didn’t win. Jesus is alive.”*

---

### Scene 18 – A Living Hope

Abi stepped closer to the empty tomb.

*“So the cross wasn’t the end…”*

*“No,”* Grammy said, smiling. *“It was the beginning.”*

Papa knelt down so they could all see.

> *“Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ… who according to His great mercy has caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.”*  
> — **1 Peter 1:3 (NASB 2020)**

*“Because Jesus rose again, everything changes,”* Papa said.

> *“Therefore if anyone is in Christ, this person is a new creation; the old things passed away; behold, new things have come.”*  
> — **2 Corinthians 5:17 (NASB 2020)**

*“Our sin is forgiven… and now we have life—real life—with Him forever.”*

He paused, then gently added:

*“And that includes every one of us… even the ones we’re still waiting to meet.”*

Samuel smiled. *“You mean Eliana?”*

Papa nodded.

> *“Before I formed you in the womb I knew you.”*  
> — **Jeremiah 1:5 (NASB 2020)**

*“Yes. Before she ever takes her first step on this trail… Jesus has already made the way for her.”*

Abi’s face softened.

*“That means this story… it’s still going.”*

Papa smiled.

> *“I am the resurrection and the life; the one who believes in Me will live, even if he dies.”*  
> — **John 11:25 (NASB 2020)**

*“Exactly. And now we walk it with Him.”*
